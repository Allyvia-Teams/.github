## The Problem

Today's POS systems record sales, but they don't help fashion retailers predict demand.

Store owners are constantly making inventory decisions based on instinct and delayed signals. The cost of getting it wrong is high: stockouts drive missed revenue, while excess inventory leads to markdowns and cash tied up in products that don't move.

## What Allyvia Does

Allyvia combines POS and AI forecasting to help fashion retailers understand demand in real time and predict what to do next.

- **Capture the source of truth** — Centralizes transactions, inventory, and product data in one system
- **Predict demand, not just sales** — Combines POS data with external signals (weather, events, foot traffic) to forecast what styles, sizes, and products will sell next
- **Turn insights into action** — Clear recommendations on what to reorder, what's slowing down, and where revenue is at risk

The result: stores move from reactive reporting to proactive decision-making.

## Business Model

| Path | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Full Allyvia** | Monthly platform fee + transaction-based revenue for stores using Allyvia for checkout, inventory, and store planning |
| **Piggyback** | Lighter entry point — retailers keep their existing POS and use Allyvia for forecasting, inventory, and operations via integrations |

## Platform

| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| **POS** | Process transactions, manage products and variants, tie every sale to data |
| **Inventory** | Track what's selling, what's slowing, what needs reordering |
| **AI Forecasting** | Demand forecasting using store data + external signals |
| **Team Management** | Role-based access, invite flows, staffing tools |
| **Integrations** | QuickBooks, Square, Stripe — built in |
| **Insights** | Actionable reports and recommendations, not just dashboards |
